Highest Purchase Cost

Keep product cost aligned with the highest validated purchase price

Highest Purchase Cost adds a costing method for inventory valuation scenarios where products must never be costed below the highest unit purchase price received.

What it does

  • Adds Highest Purchase Price as a cost method on products, product categories, and companies.
  • Updates the standard cost when validated incoming purchase stock moves show a higher unit price.
  • Keeps outgoing valuation based on the current highest purchase cost.
  • Works with Odoo Inventory, Purchase, and Accounting stock valuation flows.

Typical use cases

  • Businesses that need conservative replacement-cost tracking.
  • Companies selling items with volatile vendor prices.
  • Inventory teams that want purchase receipts to raise product cost only when the received unit cost is higher.

Configuration

  1. Install the module.
  2. Open a product category, product, or company inventory settings.
  3. Select Highest Purchase Price as the cost method.
  4. Validate purchase receipts normally.

Translations

The module includes translations for Spanish, French, and Portuguese. English is used as the source language.
